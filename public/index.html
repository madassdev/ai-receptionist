Questions

What business owners usually ask

Could it book a time when nobody is free?

No. The AI can only offer times your system says are free, and the booking system itself refuses any slot that's already taken, even if two customers try at the same second. You can test that in "Try to break it" above.

What if a customer asks something it shouldn't answer?

It answers from your price list and policies only. Anything outside that (complaints, emergencies, billing disputes) gets handed to your team with the customer's details.

Can customers change or cancel their own bookings?

Yes, with their booking reference and the last four digits of their phone number, so nobody can change someone else's appointment.

How much does it cost to run?

Usually a few cents per conversation in AI usage. Daily spending caps mean a busy or spammy day can never run up a surprise bill.

Does it work with my existing software?

This demo uses its own calendar. For your business it connects to what you already use, such as Google Calendar, Jobber, ServiceM8, Housecall Pro or a CRM like GoHighLevel.
